FAQ

Quick answers.

How fast can I get my job?

Standard 3–5 working days. Express 48hrs available for flyers, cards, banners and social kits.

Do you deliver outside Ikeja / Lagos?

Yes. We dispatch nationwide via trusted logistics and share tracking same day.

Will colours match what I see on my phone?

Yes — we send a digital proof + physical proof on large runs. Our 100% colour-match promise covers reprint if we miss.

What do you need from me to start?

Just your logo (if any), text, pictures and size/qty. Don’t have a logo? We’ll design one first.
